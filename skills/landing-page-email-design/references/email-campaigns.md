# Match email to the landing-page promise

Before setup, identify the source form, promised resource, sender, audience consent/status, campaign versus newsletter, timing and desired next action. Check existing automation and suppressions before adding another sequence.

## Plan the journey

| Possible message | Reader need | Useful content |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery/welcome | Receive what they requested | Resource or access link, what to do first and what follow-up to expect |
| First useful result | Make progress without overwhelm | One practical step or short demonstration |
| Objection/help | Resolve a real hesitation | Honest answer, example and relevant limitations |
| Decision | Choose an appropriate next step | Clear offer, truthful terms and one main CTA |

These are optional roles, not a required four-email sequence. Choose cadence for the promise and audience. Do not invent an expiring offer to justify urgency.

For each selected email, draft its audience condition, subject, preview text, body, CTA destination, delay and success event. Keep subject/body promises consistent. Preview text should add context rather than repeat the subject. Do not use misleading reply prefixes, fake personal familiarity or invented customer stories.

Use readable text and a simple layout, meaningful link labels, checked personalization fallback and the required unsubscribe/sender information. Images should support the message; essential instructions should remain readable when images are blocked.

## Set up and verify

Use current platform documentation for sender authentication, campaign activation/delays and form triggers. Skill installation alone cannot configure an email account. Separate drafting, saving, testing, scheduling and broadcasting. Sending to a list requires authorization for that audience and action.

Test only to an approved recipient. Inspect content, variables, CTA, delivery evidence and the actual subscription trigger. Avoid sending several live messages merely to test timing. Record any behavior that cannot be checked safely.

Measure the business outcome, not only opens: completed download, qualified enquiry, enrolment or purchase, as appropriate. Consider complaints/unsubscribes and delivery problems alongside clicks. Small samples and audience differences can make apparent improvements unreliable.
