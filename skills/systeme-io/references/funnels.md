# Funnels and landing pages

Translate the goal into stages: visitor -> opt-in or purchase -> confirmation -> delivery. Choose an audience-building, selling or custom route based on the desired action. Verify the plan before choosing evergreen webinars.

Read [funnel creation](https://help.systeme.io/article/147-how-to-create-a-funnel) and [opt-in configuration](https://help.systeme.io/article/152-how-to-create-a-opt-in-page). Inspect the selected funnel's real ID, domain, currency, steps and template before editing. Currency and payment settings are business decisions; do not infer them from the agent's location.

Draft a single clear promise and the information a visitor needs to decide. Use authentic logos/assets, evidence for claims, readable sections, intentional icons and mobile spacing. Preserve working referral parameters when requested. Do not invent discounts or fabricate testimonials.

For forms, verify field mapping, submission action, confirmation destination and automation trigger. For selling, continue to payments. A visual button is not proof of a working purchase or subscription path. Webinars require an actual schedule/content/delivery setup; a landing page alone is insufficient.

Before a full page replacement, inspect the tool's required template and export existing content. Fill every required field rather than mixing incomplete HTML with the page model.

Verify all step URLs, transitions and available tracking settings, then the public rendering. Test only the authorized opt-in/purchase route. Do not submit real payments merely to test a page. Report any untested checkout stage.

Find advanced sources in [Sales funnels](https://help.systeme.io/category/146-sales-funnel): order bumps, upsells, coupons, A/B tests and webinar instructions. Read the exact relevant article first.
