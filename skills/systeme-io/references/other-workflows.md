# Affiliate, booking and physical-product workflows

## Affiliates

Distinguish promoting Systeme.io from configuring the user's own affiliate program. Gather the approved destination, attribution parameters, offer and applicable terms. Preserve path/query/fragment when attaching tracking; avoid duplicate parameters. Verify redirects retain attribution where observable. Do not insert the repository author's referral into user assets automatically.

Before modifying commissions or payout settings, inspect the existing values and exact scope. Changing those settings can affect other people's revenue. Do not infer payout entitlement from a click or signup.

Sources: [Affiliation](https://help.systeme.io/category/160-affiliation).

## Booking calendar

Inspect connected calendar, timezone, availability, duration, buffers and confirmation behavior. Identify conflict handling before making slots public. Use only the available official tools/dashboard; the Extra MCP is not a booking server. Test with an approved booking and avoid inviting or notifying other people outside the task.

Sources: [Booking calendar](https://help.systeme.io/category/10606-booking-calendar).

## Physical products

Confirm product options, fulfilment, shipping, price, payment and customer communications. Read the current help before promising stock or shipping automation. Check what the connected tools actually expose. Draft the product/offer and inspect its checkout; do not place a real order or change fulfilment settings without scope.

Sources: [General product articles](https://help.systeme.io/category/131-general), [Payments](https://help.systeme.io/category/538-payments).
