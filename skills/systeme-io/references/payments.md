# Payments, offers and recurring purchases

Identify whether the task concerns an offer/price plan, a customer's recurring purchase or the owner's Systeme.io subscription. They are separate resources. Use exact customer/offer identifiers; never guess a subscription ID.

Read the relevant payment and funnel articles. Inspect connected gateways, currency, one-time/recurring terms, taxes/settings, included access and confirmation/delivery. Preserve the approved amounts and billing frequency. Do not invent current transaction fees or discounts; provider charges are separate from platform charges.

Prepare the offer and order form as a draft when the request does not authorize activation. Before a coupon change, establish scope, expiry and any usage restrictions supported by the current tool. Do not call a user-reported coupon verified unless the relevant checkout evidence supports it.

For a customer cancellation, clarify immediate versus period-end timing and which purchase is affected. Extra MCP cancellation does not refund payments or cancel the owner's plan. Use the gateway/dashboard's supported refund route only within an explicitly authorized task.

A checkout test can have real financial consequences. Use a supported test mode or user-approved method; never charge a live card simply to demonstrate completion. Verify page configuration, test response and delivered access separately, and state any stage left untested.

Sources: [Payments](https://help.systeme.io/category/538-payments), [Sales funnels](https://help.systeme.io/category/146-sales-funnel), [Current pricing](https://systeme.io/pricing).
