# Build a landing page that stands out and converts

Use this after the brief and copy exist ([audience and copy](audience-copy.md)) and before editing the page ([sales funnels](../funnels.md) or [websites](../websites.md)).

## 1. Decide before styling

1. **Subject first.** Name the product, the reader and the page's one job. Take visuals from the subject's own world: the real product, real screenshots, the real price, the real course modules.
2. **Token plan.** Pick 4–6 named colors with roles (ground, text, muted text, accent, primary button, alert), one heading face and one body face, and a spacing scale. Ask: "Would this look the same on any other site?" If yes, change something specific to the subject.
3. **One bold element.** Make one thing big and memorable, usually the product image or the outcome headline. Keep everything else quiet.
4. **Show 2–3 directions first** when the look is open: a short wireframe or mockup for each, with a recommendation. Build only the chosen one. Skip this for an exact fix the user already specified.

## 2. Page skeleton

| Block | Job | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | "Is this for me?" | Outcome headline, one line of who/what, product visual, one primary button |
| Problem → result | "Why should I care?" | The reader's real situation in their words, then what changes |
| What you get | "What exactly is it?" | 3–5 concrete items, each starting with a noun, each with its own meaning icon |
| Proof | "Why believe it?" | Real screenshots, demos, the creator's own results or attributable quotes. Never invented testimonials or numbers |
| Offer | "What does it cost and what happens next?" | Price or free route, real terms and timing, one button |
| FAQ | "What still worries me?" | Answers to the actual objections found in research |
| Final CTA | "OK, how do I start?" | Same button wording and destination as the hero |

Drop blocks the page does not need; a link-in-bio or opt-in page may need only hero, what you get and the form.

## 3. Offer and CTA blocks

- **Show the product.** A real mockup or screenshot, large, overlapping or bleeding past the panel edge. A text-only offer box underperforms and looks generic.
- **Lead with the outcome** in the headline ("Get 8 ready-made courses to sell"), not a question ("Want something special?").
- **Value stack:** 3 concrete items with icons that match their meaning (gift = free, credit card = paid, mail = emails, play = video).
- **Price as a choice** when there is a free route: paid price vs free route side by side, with honest conditions ("delivered 30 days after you sign up").
- **One primary button** per screen: solid, high contrast, in a color used nowhere else on the page. Use a specific verb + object ("See the 8 courses"). Buttons that open another page end with an arrow icon.
- **Contrast with neighbors:** give the offer block a different ground from the sections around it so it reads as the decision point.
- **Mobile:** image on top and still large, button full width.

## 4. Forms

- Put the form where the reader has enough information to decide, and repeat it at the end of long pages.
- Ask only for what follow-up needs (often first name + email). Every field has a visible label, not only a placeholder.
- Under the button, say what happens next and when ("The guide arrives by email in a minute").
- In Systeme.io, keep all fields inside one **Form** element and point the automation trigger at that form in **Form element** mode (see [automation and contacts](../automation-contacts.md)).

## 5. Anti-template checklist

Fail any item → fix before showing the user.

- [ ] Every list item, price option, feature and button has an icon that means that exact thing. Plain ticks only for yes/no or pros/cons.
- [ ] Not a stack of identical rounded cards with a thin border, soft shadow and a colored left stripe.
- [ ] No ALL-CAPS tracked labels used as decoration; no decorative 01/02/03 numbering; no "A · B · C" meta strings.
- [ ] No gradient wash used only as decoration; a gradient must spotlight something.
- [ ] No highlighting a single word with color or italics as a gimmick.
- [ ] At most one purposeful motion; respect reduced-motion.
- [ ] Borders, rules and labels carry information or are removed.
- [ ] Platform styles are reset inside custom blocks (for example inherited highlights on bold text).
- [ ] No fake urgency timers, invented scarcity, fake reviews or unverified "#1" claims.

## 6. Usability floor

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| Text | Body 16px or more, line height 1.5–1.75, about 65–75 characters per line |
| Contrast | 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text; never color alone to carry meaning |
| Targets | Buttons and links at least 44px tall on mobile, 8px apart, visible hover and focus |
| Layout | No sideways scroll at 375px; reserve image space with width/height or aspect ratio |
| Numbers | Prices and tables use aligned figures; savings calculated from verified prices |
| Images | Descriptive alt text; compressed; lazy-load below the fold |

## 7. Check the real page

Screenshot the published page at desktop and 375px wide (and dark mode if the template supports it). Click every button, submit the form once with an authorized test address, then confirm the thank-you page and the first email match the promise. Remove one unnecessary element before handing over.
